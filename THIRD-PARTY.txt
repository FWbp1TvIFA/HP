4. Cello notes by flcellogrl on Freesound ("Real cello notes"), from tonejs-instruments by Nicholaus Brosowsky
   (sounds/cello): the cello sound on the piano page.
   Creative Commons Attribution 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
   https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
   Shortened, made mono and saved as smaller files. See sounds/cello/LICENSE.txt.

Ideas, not code: the falling notes follow the style of open source piano trainers such as MIDIano
(https://github.com/Bewelge/MIDIano), and the way of hearing a note from the microphone is the McLeod
pitch method, as also used by PitchDetect (https://github.com/cwilso/PitchDetect).
