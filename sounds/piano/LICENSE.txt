Piano sounds in this folder

These recordings come from the Salamander Grand Piano (a Yamaha C5 grand) by Alexander Holm,
shared under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
They were taken from the audio collection of the Tone.js project (https://github.com/Tonejs/audio, folder "salamander").

Changes made for Happy Home: each note was shortened with a fade-out, made mono, made a little louder and
saved again as a smaller MP3 file. One note is kept for every three keys; the keys in between are tuned from it.
